# Hindi and Kannada: reviewer pack

Generated 2026-10-28. The script of every text is machine-checked (no stray letters from another script); **the wording is not, and that is what we need you for.**

Medicine names, ingredient names, numbers and units (mg, ml) stay as printed on the strip, in Latin script, on purpose.

## How to review

For each row or item mark: **meaning** (says the same as the English), **dose or safety** (nothing wrong or missing: **a wrong dose or a softened warning is a blocking error**), **plain** (simple words an ordinary reader knows; no wrong-language words), **natural** (sounds like a pharmacist, not a translation). Write corrections in the last column.

Three kinds of text, three ways they are made:

- **Part A and Part C are fixed phrases.** No model writes them. If these are right, every dose sentence, safety note, timetable and disclaimer the app can show is right. **Please check every row.**
- **Part B is written by AI** (what each medicine is for, and what to watch for). It differs every time; a sample is below.
